! Narrow advertising media replacement
! -----------------------------------------------------

! IMPORTANT:
! This is NOT generic MP4 blocking.
!
! EasyList specifically identifies:
!
! aucdn.net/library/*.mp4
!
! as advertising media on XVideos and related sites.
!
! .2 simply blocked the request. That did not remove
! the observed pre/post ads.
!
! .3 instead replaces only this known ad-media path
! with a harmless blank/no-op MP4 resource.

||aucdn.net/library/*.mp4$media,third-party,redirect=noopmp4-1s,domain=xvideos.com|xvideos2.com

! =====================================================
! SAFETY NOTES
! =====================================================

! DO NOT add:
!
! *.mp4$media,domain=...
! *.mp4$media,third-party,domain=...
!
! Those can break legitimate playback and previews.
!
!
! A narrow proven path such as:
!
! ||known-ad-host.example/ad-path/*.mp4$media,domain=...
!
! is fundamentally different.
!
!
! DO NOT add broad cosmetic rules such as:
!
! example.com##[href*="base64"]
! example.com##[href*="data:"]
!
! without proving they are harmless.
!
!
! DO NOT disable all JavaScript on a video site.
!
! DO NOT hide a complete player/container merely because
! an advertisement appears somewhere inside it.
!
!
! If a scriptlet changes unwanted NEW-TAB behavior into
! unwanted SAME-TAB navigation, treat that experiment as
! FAILED and remove it.
!
!
! Success means:
!
! unwanted behavior gone
! AND
! normal site behavior intact.
