! SAFETY / HISTORY
! =====================================================

! NEVER restore generic rules such as:
!
! *.mp4$media,domain=...
! *.mp4$media,third-party,domain=...
!
! They previously endangered legitimate playback,
! thumbnail previews and normal player behavior.
!
!
! The narrow EasyList rule:
!
! ||aucdn.net/library/*.mp4$rewrite=abp-resource:blank-mp4,...
!
! is fundamentally different because it identifies one
! known advertising media path.
!
!
! DO NOT block all JavaScript.
!
! DO NOT hide the entire player.
!
! DO NOT hide broad video/media containers.
!
!
! FAILED EXPERIMENT:
!
! 20260811.2
! xvideos.com,xvideos2.com##+js(aeld, click, ShouldShow)
!
! On the target Vivaldi Android device this changed an
! unwanted new-tab redirect into unwanted SAME-TAB
! navigation.
!
! Do not restore that rule by itself.
!
!
! Success means BOTH:
!
! 1. unwanted behavior is gone
! 2. normal site behavior remains intact
