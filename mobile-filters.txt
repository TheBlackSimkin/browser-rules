! =====================================================
! SAFETY NOTES
! =====================================================

! DO NOT add rules such as:
!
! *.mp4$media,domain=...
! *.mp4$media,third-party,domain=...
!
! They can block legitimate playback and preview media.
!
! DO NOT add broad cosmetic rules such as:
!
! example.com##[href*="base64"]
! example.com##[href*="data:"]
!
! without proving they are harmless.
!
! DO NOT hide a complete player/container merely because an ad
! appears somewhere inside it.
!
! If something survives, identify that specific request or element
! and add one narrow rule instead.
